# App usability test: session script

> **This is a blank template.** No sessions have been run yet. No participants,
> results, or success measures are recorded here. Make a copy for each
> session and fill in the copy.

## 1. Why we're doing this

We want to watch real members use the phone app (at **/app** on the site) so we
can see where it's confusing or where something is missing. **We are testing the
app, not the person.** If someone gets stuck, the app needs fixing, not them.

## 2. Who to invite

Try to get a mix of people:

- **Newcomers:** people who are new to bowls or to the division
- **Experienced tournament players**
- **Tournament organizers and club officers**

**5 or 6 people is enough for a first round.** Most big problems show up after
the first few sessions.

Privacy and permission:

- **Ask each person's permission before the session**, and tell them what you'll
  note down.
- **Don't contact members for anyone else unless they've authorized it.** Only
  invite people yourself, or through someone who has agreed to help.
- **Don't put names in shared files without consent.** Use labels like
  "Participant 1" or "Newcomer A" instead.

## 3. Setting up

- Use **their own phone** if you can. It's what they'll really use.
- Plan on **about 20 minutes**.
- **One facilitator** reads the tasks and stays quiet the rest of the time.
- Bring **a note-taker** if you can find one, so the facilitator can just watch.
- Say something like this at the start:
  > "We're testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please **think
  > out loud**: tell me what you're looking for, what you expect to happen, and
  > anything that surprises you. You can stop at any time."
- **Don't help** unless they've been stuck for **about 2 minutes**. If you do
  help, write down what you said.
- **It's fine to stop at any time**, for them or for you.

## 4. Tasks

Read each task **out loud, exactly as written**. Don't point or hint at where to
tap. If they ask "Where do I go?", answer: "Wherever you'd look first."

| # | Read aloud |
|---|---|
| A | "Find a tournament coming up that you'd be eligible to play in." |
| B | "For that tournament, find the entry deadline, the entry fee, and how to enter." |
| C | "Save that tournament, then go back to the first screen and find it again." |
| D | "Find who won the most recent tournament." |
| E | "Find a club near you and how to contact it." |
| F | *(Optional, for organizers)* "Find where the latest tournament updates (like a venue change) are shown." |

## 6. After all the sessions

1. **List problems that 2 or more people ran into first.** Those are the real
   ones.
2. **Separate "confusing" from "missing."**
   - *Confusing:* it's there, but people couldn't find it or understand it.
   - *Missing:* people looked for something the app doesn't have.
3. **Don't change the app just because one person prefers something.** Note it,
   and watch for it in the next round.
4. **Share a short summary with the app maintainer.** Include the problems,
   how many people hit each one, and a quote or two. Leave out names unless
   people agreed to be named.
