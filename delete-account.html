Delete your account and data

Southwest Bowls app, by the Southwest Bowls Division (Southwest Division of Bowls USA)

You can ask us to delete your Southwest Bowls app account and the personal data attached to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to webmaster@swlawnbowls.org with the subject Delete my Southwest Bowls app account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to the app, so we can match it to your account.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

The quickest way is in the app: go to More, then App settings, then Delete my account and data, and confirm. If you are signed in, the app deletes your account straight away and then clears everything stored on your phone. If it cannot reach our server, it tells you so and leaves your phone as it was; try again later, or email us at the address above.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, including the email address used to sign in. That email address can sign up again later if you want a new account.
  • The link between your account and your membership record, so the app no longer shows your membership details to anyone.
  • Any contact-change requests you sent that are still waiting for review.
  • Everything the app keeps on your phone (saved events, followed clubs, interests, profile text and photo, text size and theme). This is cleared when you delete from the app. If you ask by email instead, uninstall the app or clear its data to remove it.

What we keep

  • Published tournament results. Players’ names in published results are the Division’s public record and are not removed.
  • The Division’s membership roster held by the Division is its own record, separate from the app account, and is not changed by deleting the app account. This includes any contact detail you asked us to change that the Division has already approved and added to the roster. To have your roster record changed or removed, email us; the Division decides, because it is the Division’s membership record.
  • A short activity log. We keep a log of actions on membership records (for example, that a membership link or a contact change was requested, and when). It holds no email address or contact detail, but it can still refer to your membership record.
  • Backups. Copies of the database made by our hosting provider may keep deleted data for a short time before they are overwritten.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as payment records for a paid tournament entry, if any.

Questions

Email webmaster@swlawnbowls.org. See also our Privacy Policy.
