Delete your account and data

Southwest Bowls app, by the Southwest Bowls Division (Southwest Division of Bowls USA)

You can ask us to delete your Southwest Bowls app account and the personal data attached to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to webmaster@swlawnbowls.org with the subject Delete my Southwest Bowls app account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to the app, so we can match it to your account.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

You can also open the app, go to More, then App settings, then Delete my account and data. That clears everything stored on your phone. For a signed-in account it also asks us to delete the account, and if that does not complete the app will tell you to email us at the address above.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, including the email address used to sign in.
  • The link between your account and your membership record, so the app no longer shows your membership details to anyone.
  • Any contact or profile details you added in the app, and any change requests you sent.
  • Everything the app keeps on your phone (saved events, followed clubs, interests, profile text and photo, text size and theme).

What we keep

  • Published tournament results. Players’ names in published results are the Division’s public record and are not removed.
  • The Division’s membership roster held by the Division is its own record, separate from the app account, and is not changed by deleting the app account.
  • Backups. Deleted data may remain in routine backups for up to 7 days, then it is removed.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as payment records for a paid tournament entry, if any.

Questions

Email webmaster@swlawnbowls.org. See also our Privacy Policy.
