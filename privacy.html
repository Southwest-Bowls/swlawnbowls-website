Southwest Bowls Division

Privacy Policy

Southwest Bowls app · Last updated October 28, 2026

Who we are

This app is run by the Southwest Bowls Division (“SWD”, “we”), the lawn bowls division covering Southern California. Questions about your privacy: webmaster@swlawnbowls.org.

If you use the app without an account

We do not ask for or store anything about you. Your saved events, followed clubs, interests, text size and theme stay on your phone only. Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) sees your IP address in routine server logs.

If you create an account

We keep your email address. If you choose to add them, we also keep your name, club and phone number. If your email matches the SWD membership roster, we show you your own membership details (club, dues status, novice date). Nobody else sees them in the app.

Your email is used to send you a sign-in code. We do not send marketing or newsletters from the app.

What stays on your phone

Your profile text, honors, photo (including the photo on your membership card), saved events, followed clubs, interests and profile design are kept on your phone, not on our servers.

Where member data is kept

Member data is held in a private database run for SWD (Supabase, United States). SWD administrators who maintain the roster can see it. Our service providers that run the database and send sign-in emails process it for us under contract.

Public results

Names of players already appear in published tournament results, as they always have. Deleting your account does not remove published results, which are the Division’s record.

Maps

The clubs map uses free OpenFreeMap tiles (data from OpenStreetMap). Opening the map sends the area you are viewing, and your IP address as for any web request, to OpenFreeMap. No personal details are sent.

Contact form

If you use the contact form in the app, the name, email address and message you type are sent to the Southwest Division through a Google Form, and Google processes them as our service provider. We use them only to answer you.

Videos, draws and entry lists

Southwest TV videos are played from YouTube, and tournament draws and entry lists are shown from Google Sheets. When you open them, YouTube or Google receives your IP address and the usual details of a web request, as with any website. We do not send them your name or email.

No tracking, no ads

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app at any time.

To delete your account and data, open the app, go to More, then App settings, then Delete my account and data, and confirm. A signed-in account is deleted straight away, and then everything stored on your phone is cleared. You can also email webmaster@swlawnbowls.org from the address you signed in with, and we delete the account within 30 days. The page Delete your account lists exactly what is deleted and what we keep: published results, the Division’s membership roster, a short activity log, and database backups that our hosting provider may keep for a short time.

California residents may ask to know, correct or delete their personal information using the same contact.

Children

The app is for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not ask for ages. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if anything changes.
